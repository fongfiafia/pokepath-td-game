## <a id="core-mechanics"></a>Core Mechanics: What The Game Doesn't Tell You

Before you place your first tower, you need to unlearn some habits from traditional Pokémon games. PokePath TD operates on its own set of rules.

### 1. No Elemental Type Matchups
This is the most common mistake new players make. In PokePath TD:
*   **Water** does NOT deal extra damage to **Fire**.
*   **Electric** does NOT deal extra damage to **Flying**.

Damage is calculated purely based on **Attack Power**, **Attack Speed**, **Crit Rate**, and special traits (like Anti-Armor). Do not build a "balanced type team"; build a team with balanced *roles* (DPS, Slow, Stun, Economy).

### 2. The "Ditto" Leveling Secret
**Ditto** is the most broken unit in the game, but not for the reason you think.
*   **The Mechanic:** When Ditto transforms, it copies the target's species and moves, but it keeps its **OWN Level**.
*   **The Strategy:** If you have a Level 100 Ditto and it copies a Level 1 Weavile, you get a **Level 100 Weavile** on the field.
*   **Conclusion:** You do not need to level up your entire team. Prioritize getting Ditto to Level 100 first, and it effectively levels up your entire roster.

### 3. Mid-Wave Evolution
Pokemon gain experience as they deal damage. You can evolve them mid-wave by clicking on them when they reach the level threshold. Evolution resets the attack timer but massively boosts range and damage. Time your evolutions to clutch difficult waves.

## <a id="the-unit-tier-list"></a>The Unit Tier List (Meta Defined)

Forget your favorites from the anime. In PokePath TD, utility is king.

### T0: The "Must Haves" (Economy & Core)
*   **Persian:** The engine of your success. Its ability **Pay Day** generates gold equal to 10% of the enemy's value on *every hit*. Attack speed is the only stat that matters here.
*   **Ditto:** The universal tool. Use it to copy Persian for farming, or copy your main DPS for boss killing.

### T1: The Meta Carries
*   **Weavile:** The single-target DPS king. High attack speed and critical hit rate make it the best unit for melting high-HP bosses like **Registeel** and **Suicune**.
*   **Corsola:** The best support unit in the game. It has a **circular range** (rare in this game), deals splash damage, and slows enemies. It fits on almost every map.
*   **Maractus:** The "Grass Tile" nuke. If you can place it on a grass tile, its AOE damage output outclasses almost everything else for clearing waves.

### T2: The Specialists
*   **Excadrill:** **Essential for Wave 90+.** It is the best Anti-Armor unit in the game. You need this to clear the swarms of Rhydons in the late game.
*   **Shuckle:** At Level 100, Shuckle's attack cooldown matches its slow duration, creating a **Perma-Slow** effect. Vital for bosses immune to Stun (like Zapdos).
*   **Honchkrow:** The most reliable Anti-Stealth unit. High damage and good range.
*   **Gengar:** Uses Poison/Nightmare to deal percentage-based damage. Essential for the final boss of Route 3-3.

### The "Trap" Tier (Avoid Investing)
*   **Spinda:** Fun in the early game due to rapid-fire attacks, but its damage falls off a cliff in the late game. Do not rely on it for Wave 80+.
*   **Fossil Teams:** While cool, full fossil teams (Kabutops, Armaldo, etc.) generally underperform compared to a mixed meta team.

## <a id="enemy-types-counters"></a>Enemy Types & Counters (Why You Are Losing)

If you are leaking lives, it is likely due to one of these three enemy types:

### 1. Invisible (Stealth) Enemies
*   **The Threat:** **Kecleon**. Standard towers cannot target them. They will walk right past your defense.
*   **The Counter:** You **must** have a detection unit.
    *   **Best:** Honchkrow, Xatu.
    *   **Niche:** Grumpig (can be placed on grass).
    *   **Pro Tip:** Splash damage (from Corsola) can hit invisible enemies if they are standing next to a visible enemy.

### 2. High Armor Enemies
*   **The Threat:** **Rhydon**, **Bastiodon**, **Onix**. These enemies take significantly reduced damage from standard attacks.
*   **The Counter:** **Defense Breakers**.
    *   **Best:** Excadrill (AOE Armor Break), Machamp (Single Target).
    *   **Strategy:** Set these units to target "Highest Armor".

### 3. Boss Immunities
Not all bosses are the same.
*   **Articuno (Route 1-1):** Can be Stunned. (Use Ampharos/Lapras).
*   **Zapdos (Route 1-2):** **Immune to Stun.** You MUST use **Slow** (Ferrothorn/Shuckle/Tangrowth).
*   **Suicune (Route 2-3):** Very fast movement speed. Requires high burst DPS.

## <a id="infinite-money-strategy"></a>The "Infinite Money" Farming Strategy

To afford Level 100 upgrades (which cost millions), you cannot just play normally. You need to **Farm the Boss**.

### The Setup
1.  **Team:** Persian (Slot 1), Ditto (Slot 2), and 4-5 Slow/Stun units (Shuckle, Ferrothorn, etc.).
2.  **Settings:** Go to Settings and change "Auto Restart" to **"Retry"**.

### The Method
1.  Play until **Wave 100** (The Boss Wave).
2.  Your goal is **NOT** to kill the boss quickly.
3.  Your goal is to **Stun/Slow the boss** and let your **Persian + Ditto (copied Persian)** hit it as many times as possible.
4.  **Let the boss escape (or kill you).**
5.  Because you set the game to "Retry", the wave will reset. You keep the gold you earned, but the boss respawns with full HP.
6.  Repeat this loop indefinitely (AFK).

### Farming Progression Ladder
*   **Early Game:** Farm **Articuno** (Route 1-1). ~120k Gold per run.
*   **Mid Game:** Farm **Zapdos** (Route 1-2). ~270k Gold per run. (Remember: Use Slows, not Stuns).
*   **End Game:** Farm **Suicune** (Route 2-3). ~300k-500k Gold per run.

## <a id="save-data-guide"></a>Save Data & Technical Guide

**Critical Warning:** PokePath TD is a browser game. Clearing your browser cache/cookies will **delete your save file forever**.

### How to Save Correctly
1.  **Force Autosave:** The game saves when you switch maps. Always click "Map" and switch to a different route before closing the tab.
2.  **Export Data:** Go to Settings -> Export Data. **Click Download**. Do not just copy the text (it can be too long for clipboard). Keep this `.txt` file on your computer.

### iOS / Mobile Issues
iPhone users often report the game getting stuck at 99% loading or saves wiping upon refresh.
*   **Solution:** The browser version is not fully optimized for iOS memory management. We highly recommend playing on a **Desktop PC** or **Android** device for a stable experience. If you must play on iOS, export your save manually after *every* session.
